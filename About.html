About Checkmate

We believe that every shopkeeper, no matter the size, deserves access to smart, simple, and powerful tools. Checkmate was born from a simple idea: inventory management shouldn't be a chore.

We can help you manage inventory for many things like Groceries, Fashion, Electronics, Cafes, Flower Shops and Many More.

Our mission is to empower local businesses by turning complex data into clear, actionable insights. With our AI-powered tools, you can stop guessing and start knowing exactly what to order, when to promote, and how to grow your business.

From the corner grocery store to the local fashion boutique, Checkmate is your partner in efficiency.
